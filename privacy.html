Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Truesum is built to work without sending your financial data anywhere it doesn't need to go. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Receipts are read on your iPhone

  • Receipt photos you take or choose, and the details read from them (store, items, prices, totals), are processed on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Your expenses, income, budgets and categories are stored on your iPhone.
  • To help you check a scan that went wrong, Truesum keeps a history of your recent scan photos and their results on your iPhone for up to 30 days. It never leaves your device unless you choose Report a scan problem and send it yourself (for example by email). You can delete this history anytime in Settings > Privacy.

What we collect

  • Purchases. If you subscribe to Truesum Pro, Apple processes the payment, and our subscription provider RevenueCat receives the purchase record and an anonymous app user ID to confirm your subscription. We never see your card details.
  • Diagnostics. If you share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us anonymous crash and performance reports.

What we do not collect

  • We don't upload your receipt photos or the data read from them.
  • We don't access your bank or card accounts. Truesum has no bank login.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising or tracking.

Third-party services

  • Apple (App Store, StoreKit) for purchases.
  • RevenueCat for subscription status. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Supabase to download the shared category list and which app features are turned on. No personal or receipt data is sent.

Your choices

  • Camera, Photos and notification access can be changed anytime in iOS Settings > Truesum.
  • Delete all your data in the app: Settings > Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes it from your iPhone.

Children

Truesum is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change affects how your receipt photos are handled, the app will ask you before it applies.
