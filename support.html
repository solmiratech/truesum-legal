A receipt didn't scan right

In the app, go to Settings > Privacy > Scan history on this iPhone, choose the scan, and tap Report a scan problem. You'll see exactly what will be sent before you send it.

Subscriptions

  • Restore a purchase: open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel or change your plan: iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Truesum.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your data

Settings > Delete All Data removes everything Truesum stored on your iPhone.
