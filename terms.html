2. The service

Truesum reads receipt photos you provide (camera or photo library) on your iPhone to check the bill's math and record your expenses. Receipt reading happens on your device; Truesum does not upload your receipt photos.

3. Subscriptions

  • Truesum Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, billed monthly or yearly at the price shown in the app before you buy.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. If you start a free trial, you are charged when the trial ends unless you cancel before then.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your Apple Account settings. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.
  • The free plan includes 5 scans a month.
